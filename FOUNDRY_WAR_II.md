# Foundry War II — Async Field

**Status:** live shared play record  
**Opened:** 2026-09-29

This document is a lightweight asynchronous play surface for a new Foundry war.

It is not a specification, balance document, canonical feature list, or instruction to modify the Foundry executable. Foundry's executable and semantic surface retain their existing authority. This record preserves moves, consequences, shared handles, surprises, and interpretations as the war develops.

## Play discipline

A turn may introduce an action, construction, creature, machine, event, tactic, environmental change, or other move that can be understood in the shared Foundry world.

Do not require every move to correspond to implemented machinery. Do not silently promote play vocabulary into Foundry semantic authority. If play later earns an executable experiment, that can happen separately.

Keep the chronology intact. Later interpretation may revise what an earlier move meant without rewriting the earlier move.

The human and model may play asynchronously by appending turns here. A turn can be terse. Shared vocabulary is allowed to emerge through use rather than definition.
